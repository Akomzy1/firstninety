Step 4 of 4 — What we'll remember

Here's what FirstNinety knows about you so far.

A short list. You can change any line, anytime.

  • You're starting as a Business Analyst.
  • You start on Monday.
  • You're working in financial services.
  • You're hybrid.

You can edit any of this anytime in Settings → What FirstNinety knows. We'll add more as you tell us things — never by guessing, never by reading anything we shouldn't.

We will not store the names of your colleagues, your manager, your stakeholders, or your employer. When you paste anything from work into FirstNinety, we'll prompt you to anonymise first.
