DebriefThe Hostile Lead Dev · 11 turns · 14 min

You held the room. Just.

You made it through eleven turns without losing the workshop. That's harder than it sounds for a first session. Three things to take away.

Green flags

1.You redirected Sam's first objection without dismissing it. That's hard. Most new BAs either capitulate or get defensive.

2.You used an open question to pull Priya into specifics. She gave you something to work with.

Yellow flags

1.When Marcus surfaced the compliance question on turn 4, you moved past it. In a real workshop that question will come back, often via email to your manager. Learn to land the compliance flag in the room.

2.You over-relied on Priya for direction. She doesn't have it. Your role is to draw it out, not wait for it.

Not yet

1.You didn't manage Sam's frustration — you just routed around it. That works once. Not twice.

What this scenario rehearses for

This is the dynamic you'll meet in any requirements workshop where one stakeholder thinks the meeting is beneath them. The script doesn't change much. The personalities do.
