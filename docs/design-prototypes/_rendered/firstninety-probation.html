Probation Mode — invitation, not interruption.

Three quiet moments. The day FirstNinety notices a probation review is three weeks away and asks, in passing, whether to focus the work. The settings page where the date lives, edited by hovering a sentence — not by filling a form. And the empty version of that page for the user whose role doesn't have a probation review at all.
